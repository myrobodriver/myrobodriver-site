# Credits

## MyRoboDriver brand artwork

These are MyRoboDriver’s own brand assets, supplied by Darrell (the owner). They are not third-party stock, and they are not Tesla press or brand assets. The car in the artwork has no badge.

The mascot was cleaned from that artwork: the white page background was removed, and the drawing was snapped to red `#C8102E`, black, and white. The wordmark is a new lockup in the same spirit as the circular badge (red script “My”, outlined “Robo”, heavy black “Driver”), set as SVG paths. Letterforms were drawn from Kaushan Script (Pablo Impallari, SIL Open Font License 1.1) and Archivo Black (Omnibus-Type, SIL Open Font License 1.1). The font files are not shipped.
